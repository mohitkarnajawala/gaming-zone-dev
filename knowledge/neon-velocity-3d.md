# Neon Velocity 3D — Game Guide

## Quick summary
Neon Velocity 3D (also known as Infinite Runner) is a fast-paced pseudo-3D endless runner set on a curving neon highway. Players maneuver across **3 lanes** (Left, Center, Right), leaping over ground hurdles and sliding under high laser barriers while gathering coins and game-changing power-ups like Shields, Magnets, and 2x Multipliers.

## How to play
1. Start the game to launch onto the 3D neon highway.
2. Swipe Left or Right (or use Left/Right arrow keys) to change lanes.
3. Swipe Up (or Up arrow key) to jump over low hurdles and road blocks.
4. Swipe Down (or Down arrow key) to duck/slide under overhead laser barriers.
5. Swipe Down while mid-air to fast-fall back to the road immediately.
6. Collect Neon Coins, Energy Orbs, and power-ups (Shield, Magnet, 2x Multiplier).
7. Avoid collisions: hitting an obstacle without a Shield ends the run.

## Game Rules
- The highway consists of 3 distinct lanes: Left, Center, and Right.
- Road curves dynamically left and right as forward speed increases with distance.
- Low hurdles require a jump; sliding into them results in a collision.
- High laser barriers require a duck/slide; jumping into them results in a collision.
- Having an active **Shield** absorbs one obstacle collision and keeps the run going.
- Unshielded collision with any barrier triggers immediate Game Over.

## Power-ups and Collectibles
- **Shield**: Grants an energy barrier that absorbs 1 collision, protecting you from Game Over.
- **Magnet**: Automatically pulls nearby coins from all 3 lanes toward you for a limited duration.
- **Score Multiplier (2x)**: Doubles all score points earned from distance and collectibles while active.
- **Neon Coins**: Awards +10 score points (+20 with multiplier) and increases total collected coins.
- **Energy Orbs**: Grants an instant burst of +50 score points (+100 with multiplier).

## Tips and Strategies
- **Default to the center lane**: Staying in the center lane gives you the quickest escape route to either the left or right lane.
- **Use the mid-air fast fall**: If you jump and suddenly spot an overhead laser ahead, swipe Down mid-air to fast-fall and slide underneath it.
- **Grab Shields first**: Shields are your safety net; prioritize grabbing a Shield over coins.
- **Let the Magnet do the work**: When Magnet is active, stay focused solely on dodging obstacles while coins are pulled in automatically.
- **Read road curvature early**: Watch the horizon where the road turns to prepare your lane switches early.

## Controls and Mechanics
- **Change Lane Left**: Swipe Left or press Left Arrow key.
- **Change Lane Right**: Swipe Right or press Right Arrow key.
- **Jump**: Swipe Up or press Up Arrow key.
- **Duck / Slide**: Swipe Down or press Down Arrow key.
- **Fast Fall**: Swipe Down while currently in mid-air.

## Objective and Scoring
- **Objective**: Travel the maximum distance along the 3D highway without crashing.
- **Score**: Continually accumulates based on distance traveled, multiplied by active power-ups and collectible pickups.
- **High Score**: Saved to your profile; beating your best score unlocks victory sounds and confetti.

## Prompts and Common Questions
- **"How do I switch lanes in Neon Velocity 3D?"**: Swipe Left or Right on your screen, or use the Left/Right keyboard arrow keys.
- **"How do I avoid high lasers in Neon Velocity 3D?"**: Swipe Down or press the Down arrow key to slide under high laser barriers.
- **"What does the Shield power-up do?"**: The Shield absorbs one obstacle collision, preventing Game Over and keeping your run alive.
- **"What does the Magnet power-up do?"**: The Magnet automatically attracts all nearby coins across all 3 lanes toward your runner.
- **"What is the fast fall feature?"**: Swiping Down while jumping causes your runner to drop back to the track immediately.
