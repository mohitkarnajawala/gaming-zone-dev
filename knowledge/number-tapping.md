# Number Tapping — Game Guide

## Quick summary
Number Tapping is a fast reaction speed game. A target number is displayed on the screen, and the player must quickly find and tap the identical number in a shuffled **3×3 grid** before time expires. The countdown time decreases as the player progresses, challenging recognition speed and accuracy.

## How to play
1. Start the Number Tapping game.
2. Look at the target number displayed at the top of the screen.
3. Search the shuffled 3x3 grid to locate the matching number.
4. Tap the matching number as quickly as possible before the timer runs out.
5. Continue finding target numbers as each successful tap generates the next target.
6. Stay alert: available time decreases as your score increases.
7. Avoid mistakes: tapping the wrong number or letting the countdown hit zero ends the game.

## Game Rules
- A single target number is shown for the player to find.
- The grid consists of shuffled numbers arranged in a 3x3 layout (9 cells total).
- The player must tap the exact cell matching the displayed target.
- The countdown timer gets progressively shorter with each correct answer.
- Tapping an incorrect number or letting the timer run out triggers Game Over.

## Tips and Strategies
- **Read the target number carefully**: Ensure you register the exact number before scanning.
- **Scan systematically**: Sweep the 3x3 grid row by row or in quadrants rather than searching erratically.
- **Confirm before tapping**: Double-check for a split second to prevent game-ending accidental taps.
- **Speed up under pressure**: As your score climbs and the timer shrinks, make your scanning movements sharper.
- **Stay relaxed**: Panicking leads to false taps; maintain a steady scanning pace.

## Controls and Mechanics
- **Grid Layout**: 3 rows by 3 columns (9 cells total).
- **Control Input**: Tap or click directly on the matching number tile.
- **Timer**: Shrinking progress bar countdown per target.

## Objective and Scoring
- **Objective**: Find and tap as many target numbers as possible before making an error or running out of time.
- **Score**: 1 point awarded per matching target tapped.
- **High Score**: Saved persistently; setting a new record triggers celebration audio and confetti.

## Prompts and Common Questions

- **"How many cells are in the Number Tapping grid?"**: Number Tapping uses a 3x3 grid containing 9 cells.
- **"Why does the timer get faster in Number Tapping?"**: Available time decreases as your score increases to test your reaction speed.
- **"What is the objective of Number Tapping?"**: To identify and tap matching numbers in the 3x3 grid under an increasingly strict time limit.
